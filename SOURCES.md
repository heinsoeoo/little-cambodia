# Geographic and activity references

- OpenStreetMap official map API, retrieved 18 October 2026: https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=104.917,11.553,104.942,11.580 . Prepared geometry is preserved in `public/data/phnom-penh-expanded.osm.json`; original tags, way IDs and node references remain. Converted using `scripts/convert-osm.py`. No edits were made to synced project sources.
- OSM building tags: https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:building and https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:building:levels . These inform classification and heights, not an exact architectural appearance.
- OSM water way 1101341005 supplies the mapped Tonlé Sap polygon and west-bank line. The inland promenade offset and stalls are game scenery.
- AKP, “Walk Street along Riverside Opens”, 2 February 2025: https://mail.vps.akp.gov.kh/post/detail/327565 . Reports Saturday/Sunday 18:00–23:00 operation and the municipality's plans for food stalls and cultural activities. This historical reported schedule is the game's configurable baseline; it is not a live event calendar.
- Attribution and ODbL license: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . Displayed in-game; source extracts are distributed with the project.

Daily vendor hours, foods, precise stall locations, character counts, faces, clothing, and all untagged building details are illustrative. No real business endorsement or individual seller identity is implied.
