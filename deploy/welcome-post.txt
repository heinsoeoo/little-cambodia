Welcome to Little Cambodia 🇰🇭❤️

My girlfriend Claraa missed home, so I started building a little piece of Cambodia we could explore together.

Walk along Phnom Penh’s riverside, meet friends, visit the street market, or summon a car, tuk-tuk, or motorcycle and see where the roads take you.

This is our first playable version, and there’s still plenty to build. Phnom Penh comes first, with Siem Reap planned for later.

Come make yourself at home: https://little-cambodia.hexdock.dev

Made with ❤️ by Hein, for Claraa—and everyone who misses Cambodia.
